Engineering trains you to focus on the details and model the world as constrained systems, mathematics convinces you that most problems can be solved by logical reasoning, and the right computational tools, once developed, empower you to act on this belief. The process of building technology require(d) humans to adopt such an approach.

Today, we are placing bets on the extent that AI can take over this process. LLMs were traditionally believed to be incapable of reasoning beyond pattern-matching, but it appears that the goalposts are shifting. With this trend, will an AI model produce better mechanical designs, better firmware, even better chips than any human?

Remember The Human explores such ideas from the perspective of a new CS graduate building safe real-time systems.
