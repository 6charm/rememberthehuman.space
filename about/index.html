Engineering trains you to model the world as constrained systems that can be optimized, mathematics convinces you that most problems can be solved by logical reasoning, and the right computational tools, once developed, empower you to act on this belief.

Unemotional observation - a necessary attitude in technical fields - is hard to sustain in a city like Mumbai; simply living here reveals a spectrum of human realities. Remember The Human explores this phenomenon in the context of day-to-day programming work.
