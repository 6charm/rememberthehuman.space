Engineering trains you to focus on the details and model the world as constrained systems, mathematics convinces you that most problems can be solved by logical reasoning, and the right computational tools, once developed, empower you to act on this belief.

Unemotional observation - a rewarding attitude in technical fields - is hard to sustain in any city; simply living reveals a spectrum of human realities. Remember The Human explores these ideas through notes on various subjects.
