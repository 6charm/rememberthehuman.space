# Intensity
@18/12/25

Its the mid-december 2025. Earlier this year, I discovered
three internet personalities that go by the names sadhguru, satya.speaks and shi-heng yi.
I have decided that "intense" must be the manner in which I do anything.

To me, intensity means to commit fully to an action ot task.  Intensity is 
resisting the urge to check your phone while eating despite no apparent reward.
i feeel intense when I'm fully focused on a task, when I grit my teeth and 
push forward. It almost feels like anger, but I am calm. When I close my eyes and
imagine intensity, I feel higher focus, and energy rising towards my head. 

Once an action is decided, the way to do it must be intensely. 
Once an action is decided, intensity enables you to carry it out with complete conviction.
The key is to make a decision - and once made, do it with conviction.
I have seen intensity eliminates fear and uncertainty in regular living.



I do not think intensity should mean having a dead serious attitude, 
or on the other hand uncontrolled emotional release.
